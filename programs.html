The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, run on your own water tests and your own tank.

Program 01 · The month that matters

Cycling & chemistry

We cycle your tank together, with daily tests logged and read week by week. Then the chemistry that matters — ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, hardness and pH — measured with a liquid test kit rather than strips, and interpreted against what you actually keep.

  • 8 weeks
  • New tanks
  • 10 min a day
  • A liquid test kit

Program 02 · Fewer fish, better tank

Stocking sensibly

Most stocking problems come from buying by appearance. We plan a stocking list around adult sizes, temperament and your water, get schooling species in proper numbers, and set up a simple quarantine routine — which prevents the disease outbreaks that wipe out established tanks.

  • 8 weeks
  • Cycled tank
  • 2 hours a week
  • A second small tank helps

Program 03 · Read the tank

Algae & problems

Algae is a symptom, usually of light and nutrients out of balance. We identify the type, fix the cause rather than dosing chemicals, and work through cloudy water, flashing, clamped fins and other early signs. Medication is a vet's territory; we focus on water quality, which solves most of it.

  • 8 weeks
  • Have a tank
  • 2 hours a week
  • Test results

Program 04 · Plants that grow

Planted tanks

Low-tech planted tanks are stable, cheap and beautiful, and they out-compete algae. We choose plants that suit your light, sort out substrate and a simple fertilizer routine, and cover trimming and layout so the tank looks composed rather than overgrown.

  • 10 weeks
  • Cycled tank
  • 3 hours a week
  • Basic lighting
